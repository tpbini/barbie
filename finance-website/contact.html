Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and process

What is the minimum investment required to work with FinancePro? +

We offer services for various investment levels. While our comprehensive wealth management services typically start at $250,000 in investable assets, we also provide financial planning consultations for those just starting their investment journey. Please contact us to discuss your specific situation.

How do you charge for your services? +

We operate on a fee-only basis, meaning we're compensated directly by our clients rather than through commissions. This typically ranges from 0.5% to 1.5% of assets under management, depending on the complexity of your situation and the services required. We also offer flat-fee and hourly consulting options for specific projects.

Are you a fiduciary? +

Yes, absolutely. FinancePro is a registered investment advisor (RIA) and is held to a fiduciary standard. This means we're legally obligated to act in your best interest at all times, putting your needs ahead of our own. We provide unbiased advice and maintain complete transparency in all our recommendations.

How often will we meet to review my portfolio? +

We typically schedule quarterly review meetings with our clients to discuss portfolio performance, rebalance investments if needed, and adjust strategies based on any changes in your life or goals. However, we're always available for additional meetings whenever significant life events occur or when you have questions or concerns.

What makes FinancePro different from other financial advisors? +

Our holistic approach sets us apart. We don't just manage investments; we create comprehensive financial plans that address all aspects of your financial life. Our team includes specialists in various areas (tax planning, estate planning, retirement planning), ensuring you receive expert guidance across all dimensions of wealth management. Plus, our 25-year track record speaks for itself.

Do you work with clients outside of New York? +

Yes! While our headquarters is in New York City, we serve clients nationwide through virtual meetings and digital platforms. We're registered to provide investment advisory services in all 50 states. Many of our clients prefer the convenience of video conferences and secure online communication for their regular meetings.
